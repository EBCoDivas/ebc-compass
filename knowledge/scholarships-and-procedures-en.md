# Guide to scholarships and school procedures

> Confirm deadlines and submit your application through Academic Services.

## Academic Excellence Scholarship (BEA)
Beca de Excelencia Académica is a publicly named EBC scholarship. Ask Academic Services about the applicable admission process and current requirements.
Check the catalog for dates for your undergraduate, executive or postgraduate program.

## Economic Support Scholarship (BRE)
Beca de Respaldo Económico is subject to assessment. The public information describes a 10% to 40% range, not a guaranteed individual benefit.
Check the catalog for your program’s deadlines.

## Applying
Ask Academic Services which documents are applicable. Check the catalog for the application deadline.
Names and general guidance: https://www.ebc.mx/becas/

## Until when can I re-enroll?
Check your Compass calendar for the re-enrollment deadline. Confirm academic periods and requirements for your modality and campus with Academic Services.

## Graduation and social service
Ask about electronic degree issuance, your academic record and social service through your campus. Check your Compass calendar for graduation and social service deadlines. Confirm the dates for your program with Academic Services.
