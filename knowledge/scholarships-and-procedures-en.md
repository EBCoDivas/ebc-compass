# Scholarships and school procedures: demo guide

> Simulated demo dates, not official application deadlines. Compass does not process applications.

## Academic Excellence Scholarship (BEA)
Beca de Excelencia Académica is a publicly named EBC scholarship. Ask Academic Services about the applicable admission process and current requirements. This demo does not specify a minimum GPA or guarantee approval.
Illustrative deadlines: undergraduate 15/12/2026, executive 16/12/2026, postgraduate 18/12/2026.

## Economic Support Scholarship (BRE)
Beca de Respaldo Económico is subject to assessment. The public information describes a 10% to 40% range, not a guaranteed individual benefit.
Illustrative deadlines: undergraduate 30/11/2026, executive 17/12/2026.

## Applying
Ask Academic Services which documents are applicable. Use only fictional paperwork in this demo. The sample application procedure ends on 28/11/2026.
Names and general guidance: https://www.ebc.mx/becas/

## Re-enrollment
The demo re-enrollment date is 20/12/2026. Actual academic periods and requirements depend on the modality and campus; check with Academic Services.

## Graduation and social service
Ask about electronic degree issuance, your academic record and social service through your campus. The demo graduation activity is dated 12/01/2027 and the social-service activity 02/12/2026. These dates are invented.
