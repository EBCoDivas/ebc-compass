# Guide to scholarships and school procedures

> Confirm deadlines and submit your application through Academic Services.

## Academic Excellence Scholarship (BEA)
Beca de Excelencia Académica is a publicly named EBC scholarship. Ask Academic Services about the applicable admission process and current requirements.
Catalog dates: undergraduate 15/12/2026, executive 16/12/2026, postgraduate 18/12/2026.

## Economic Support Scholarship (BRE)
Beca de Respaldo Económico is subject to assessment. The public information describes a 10% to 40% range, not a guaranteed individual benefit.
Catalog dates: undergraduate 30/11/2026, executive 17/12/2026.

## Applying
Ask Academic Services which documents are applicable. The catalog lists 28/11/2026 for applications.
Names and general guidance: https://www.ebc.mx/becas/

## Re-enrollment
The catalog lists 20/12/2026 for re-enrollment. Confirm academic periods and requirements for your modality and campus with Academic Services.

## Graduation and social service
Ask about electronic degree issuance, your academic record and social service through your campus. The catalog lists 12/01/2027 for graduation and 02/12/2026 for social service. Confirm the dates for your program with Academic Services.
