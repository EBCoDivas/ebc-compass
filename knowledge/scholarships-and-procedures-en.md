# Scholarships and procedures

> Simulated content for the EBC Compass demo.

## EBC Foundation Scholarship (Beca Fundación EBC)

- **Who can apply:** students from all careers, from 3rd to 8th semester.
- **Requirements:** minimum GPA of 8.5 and no outstanding debts.
- **Benefit:** covers 30% of next term's tuition.
- **Documents:** motivation letter, academic transcript and proof of enrollment.
- **Deadline:** 15/12/2026.

## Santander Excellence Scholarship (Beca Santander Excelencia)

- **Who can apply:** Finance and Banking students from 4th semester.
- **Requirements:** minimum GPA of 9.0.
- **Benefit:** $25,000 MXN applied to next term's tuition.
- **Documents:** teacher recommendation letter, academic transcript and motivation letter.
- **Deadline:** 30/11/2026.

## How to apply

1. Gather your documents as PDF files.
2. Sign in to the scholarship portal.
3. Register your application and upload the documents (deadline for the application procedure: 28/11/2026).
4. Download your receipt. Results are published 10 business days after the call closes.

## Re-enrollment

Re-enrollment for the January–May 2027 term is open until 20/12/2026. Sign in to the student portal, choose your courses and confirm online. You must have no outstanding debts and must have passed the prerequisite courses.

## Academic Merit Scholarship (Beca al mérito académico)

- **Who can apply:** students from all careers with outstanding performance.
- **Requirements:** minimum GPA of 9.5 and no outstanding debts.
- **Benefit:** institutional recognition and a 20% tuition discount for next term.
- **Documents:** academic transcript and motivation letter.
- **Deadline:** 10/12/2026.

## Study certificate (constancia de estudios)

Request a study certificate from Academic Services (Servicios Escolares). Bring your valid student ID. It is issued in 3 business days.
