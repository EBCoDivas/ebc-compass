# Frequently asked questions

> Simulated content for the EBC Compass demo.

## How do I complete my profile?
Open the Profile section and fill in your name, campus, career and semester. You will then get more accurate recommendations.

## Where can I see my important dates?
Your home screen shows the upcoming deadlines. The most urgent ones are highlighted.

## Can I save opportunities?
Yes. Open an opportunity in the catalog and choose "Save for later".

## Where do I get help?
At the Academic Services office (Servicios Escolares) on your campus, Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00.

## Does the assistant replace Academic Services?
No. The assistant guides you and summarizes information, but for official decisions always check with Academic Services.
